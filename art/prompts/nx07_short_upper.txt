THE WEAPON - BLAST KNUCKLE (right hand), exactly as in image 3. It is a GRIP-TYPE knuckle: a compact unit with a thick handle bar that the hand holds inside a closed fist. In front of the fingers it carries one wide FLAT striking plate - a thick flat rectangular slab with slightly rounded corners. The striking face is completely flat: no spikes, no points, no blades. Two copper electrode bars run across the flat face, and between them sits a row of small round detonator caps. A short armored cable connects the unit to the forearm. The unit is stored folded inside the right forearm armor; to deploy, the forearm plates slide open and the unit slides forward into the palm. Electric current runs through the electrodes (cyan-white arcs crawl over the flat face), and on impact the current sets off the detonators: an orange-and-white explosion mixed with forked cyan-white electric arcs.

THE MOVE - a short uppercut to the body (a liver shot). NX-07 steps in very close, drops low with bent knees and a twisted torso, then drives the legs and the hip upward and throws a short, compact uppercut with the right arm. The elbow stays bent at about 90 degrees and close to the body, the fist travels only a short distance upward and forward, and both feet stay on the ground. It is NOT a tall rising uppercut and the arm is never stretched straight. The flat plate of the knuckle digs into the side of the enemy's abdomen just under the ribs. On contact the knuckle detonates: the explosion and the electric arcs burst at the enemy's flank and blast out through its back. The enemy folds in half around the fist and is lifted a little off its feet.
