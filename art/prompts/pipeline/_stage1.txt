## 캐릭터 픽셀 파이프라인 — 1단계 뽑기의 공통 틀 (docs/pipeline/CHARACTER_PIPELINE.md)
## '##' 로 시작하는 줄은 설명이고 프롬프트에 들어가지 않는다. {이름} 칸은 캐릭터 설정 파일(pipeline.json)의 prompt 에서 채운다.
## NX-07 v2 1단계(nx07_side_v2_s1_common.txt)에서 통한 문장을 그대로 옮겼다 (기획서 9.11.4). 이 틀을 고치면 NX-07 재현 검증의 프롬프트 비교가 달라진다.
## v1 의 실패(9.11.3)에서 나온 금지 사항이 이 틀에 들어 있다. 지우지 않는다:
##   - 어두운 색을 막지 않는다 ("Dark colors are welcome"). v1 은 "검정은 쓰지 말라"고 적어 주인이 되는 어두운 덩어리를 스스로 막았다.
##   - 부품을 같은 무게로 나열하지 않는다 ("ONE MASTER VALUE SHAPE", "Do not chop …"). 캐릭터별 칸에도 부품 목록을 쓰지 말고 밝기 계획을 쓴다.
##   - 뻣뻣한 밑그림을 주지 않는다. 1단계에는 밑그림을 넣지 않고(도구가 막는다), 자세는 살아 있는 대기 자세로 모델이 설계한다.
##   - 형식(격자 · 색 수)만 적지 않는다. 밝기 위계와 몸짓을 적는다.
Design ONE small pixel-art game sprite of {subject} from scratch, as a pixel artist would: decide the pose, the big shapes and the light/dark plan for a tiny sprite. Do NOT shrink or trace the illustration.

REFERENCES (design only):
{references}

SIZE AND PIXELS:
- Big chunky pixels. Every art pixel is a hard square of about {s1_cell} x {s1_cell} image pixels. The whole figure is about {s1_height} art pixels tall (about {s1_height_px} image pixels), from {top_of_figure} to the soles.
- No detail smaller than one art pixel. No anti-aliasing, no gradients, no dithering, no noise or speckles, no stray single pixels.

THE FIGURE: exactly one character, side view facing RIGHT ({facing_note}), {figure}

THE POSE (a living idle stance, this drawing becomes the reference for all frames, so not an action pose):
{pose}

HOW THE BEST SMALL SPRITES OF THIS KIND ARE BUILT (follow this, it is the point of the image):
- ONE MASTER VALUE SHAPE. If you squint, the body is mostly one big connected area of a single brightness level; the largest such area covers about two thirds of the body. Do not chop the body into many medium pieces of alternating light and dark: {materials_merge} are merged into the big shape wherever they have similar brightness.
- On top of the master shape only a FEW SMALL ACCENT STROKES (1 to 3 art pixels each): {accent_strokes}. About one fifth of the cells are small strokes, the rest are big calm areas.
- Very little saturated color: only about 5% of the cells are strongly saturated. Those are {saturated}. Everything else is muted and greyish.
- NO OUTLINE: no black line drawn around the figure and no dark ring around each part. The edge of the figure is simply the edge of its big shapes. Dark parts may sit on the edge, because they are dark parts, not lines.
- Dark colors are welcome, including near-black charcoal. Each material has only 2 tones (base and shadow), plus at most one small highlight. About {colors} colors for the whole figure.
- Simplify the design for this tiny size: {simplify}

BACKGROUND: completely flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere outside the figure. No ground, no shadow, no text, no border, no grid lines.
