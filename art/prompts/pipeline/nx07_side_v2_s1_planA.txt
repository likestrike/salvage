
LIGHT AND DARK PLAN — "dark body is the master shape":
- The bodysuit is a near-black charcoal (about #22252b, shadow #16181d). The olive armor is a DARK olive drab, close to the suit in brightness (base about #4a5036, shadow #33382a). Together they form one big dark mass that fills most of the body, from the neck to the boots, including the cannon tube and the boots.
- Light accents on top of the dark mass, small and few: the pale face (the brightest area), a short mid-olive highlight band on the top of the helmet (about #6f7457), the white ring at the back end of the cannon, one small white shoulder accent.
- Cyan only on the emblem, the eye, the cannon light band and the muzzle.
