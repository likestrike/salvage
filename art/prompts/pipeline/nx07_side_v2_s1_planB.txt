
LIGHT AND DARK PLAN — "the olive armor shell is the master shape, the dark suit supports it":
- The olive armor is a calm, muted olive drab of MIDDLE brightness (base about #6f7457, shadow #4e5340, one small highlight #8a8f6c). Helmet, chest and back armor, the cannon tube, the shins and boots are all this olive and read together as one big connected olive shape that covers most of the body.
- The bodysuit is a near-black charcoal (about #24262c) that shows only in the gaps: neck, upper arms, waist and the backs of the thighs. It is the dark base that holds the olive shape together, not a separate set of pieces.
- The white plates are reduced to two small light shapes: the shoulder ball and the knee plate (off-white about #d9d6cc, shadow #a9a69c). The face is pale.
- Cyan only on the emblem, the eye, the cannon light band and the muzzle.
