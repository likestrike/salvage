THE WEAPON - BLAST KNUCKLE (right hand). It is a GRIP-TYPE knuckle: a compact unit with a thick handle bar that the hand holds inside a closed fist, like a heavy knuckle-duster. In front of the fingers it carries one wide FLAT striking plate - a thick flat rectangular slab with slightly rounded corners. The striking face is completely flat: no spikes, no points, no blades, no studs. Two copper electrode bars run across the flat face, and between them sits a row of small round detonator caps. A short armored cable connects the unit to the forearm.
How it works: the unit is stored folded inside the right forearm armor. To deploy, the forearm plates slide open and the unit slides forward on a rail into the palm; the hand grips the handle and the flat plate locks in front of the fist. Electric current then runs through the electrodes - cyan-white arcs crawl over the flat face. On impact the current sets off the detonators and the flat face erupts: an orange-and-white explosion mixed with forked cyan-white electric arcs.
