POSE GRID: 4 columns and 2 rows, eight poses.
In every pose except pose 7 the right forearm is the energy cannon. The face stays calm and serious unless the pose says otherwise.
POSES:
1. IDLE: relaxed combat stance, feet apart, knees slightly bent, the cannon held low in front of the hip, the left fist at the side.
2. RUN: the middle of a running stride, body leaning forward, the rear leg pushing off, the front knee lifted, the left arm swinging back, the cannon carried forward at waist height.
3. JUMP: in the air at the top of a jump, both knees tucked up, the cannon pointing forward, the left arm thrown back for balance.
4. SHOOT: standing firm with the feet wide, the cannon raised to chest height and aimed straight ahead, the left hand bracing the barrel; a small cyan flash at the muzzle.
5. CHARGE: a slightly lowered stance, the cannon pulled back beside the hip, a ball of cyan energy growing at the muzzle with a few small sparks.
6. DASH: lunging forward and low, the body stretched almost horizontal, a short cyan jet from the backpack, the cannon pointing ahead.
7. SHORT UPPERCUT: both feet planted on the ground, the body just risen from a crouch, the right arm (the arm nearest to the viewer) bent at the elbow and driven up in a short uppercut to chest height. In this pose only, the right arm has a normal gauntlet and a black hand instead of the cannon, and that hand grips the handle of the BLAST KNUCKLE of image 4: a flat rectangular gunmetal striking plate, wider than the fist, carried in front of the knuckles like a brick, with two copper bars and a row of round caps on its face and a short cable running back to the forearm. The striking plate must be drawn large and clearly readable - it is the point of this pose. A few small cyan sparks on the plate; mouth open in a shout.
8. HIT: knocked backward, the upper body bent back, the head thrown back, one foot leaving the ground, the arms flung loose, one eye shut in pain.
