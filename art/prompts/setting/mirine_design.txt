CHARACTER: Mirine, an 11-year-old genius girl hacker, 142 cm tall. Draw exactly this character. Do not redesign, simplify or decorate anything.

Measure the proportions on image 1 and keep them: the size of the head, the height in heads, the short child's body, the big sneakers, the length of the hair and of the coat.
- image 2 = the original design sheet of Mirine. Design reference only: do not copy its drawing style, its text or its layout.

DESIGN - keep exactly as in image 1:
- Head: long loose silver hair down to her waist, with long bangs between the eyes and one small curled ahoge on top; large teal eyes under thick upper lash lines; a small dark-teal device clipped in her hair above one ear.
- Coat: an oversized navy-blue robe coat worn open, with a hood collar, very wide sleeves, pale trim lines along the edges and across the sleeve cuffs, a white wing-shaped emblem on the upper sleeve, small gold corner marks on the cuffs, and a torn, frayed hem that is faded pale at the tears.
- Under the coat: a plain white T-shirt, rolled-up blue denim shorts, a brown leather belt with a square buckle, a brown leather tool pouch full of small tools hanging on a strap at her hip. On the back, the belt runs over the coat and carries two small teal-lit devices.
- Legs: bare legs, gray socks, chunky navy-and-white high-top sneakers with thick white soles.
- In her hand: her self-built handheld hacking terminal: a chunky rugged dark-gray handheld with a glowing teal screen above a small keypad.
- A child's body and a child's face. Nothing mature, nothing glamorous.
